About us

The dental practice is located in Charenton-le-Pont (94220).
Dr Lellouche and his team welcome you to a modern, clean and hygienic environment.
We offer all types of dental treatment, including dental implants, dental prostheses, gum treatment and cosmetic dentistry.
We are committed to giving you the best information and care regarding your dental treatment.
Please note that we speak English.

New patients

Your first consultation will begin with a discussion of any dental concerns you may have.

At your first appointment, we ask you to complete a medical history form. This ensures that, before we examine your mouth, we are aware of any medical factors that could affect your treatment. We then carry out a full examination of your teeth, jaw and the soft tissues inside your mouth, and take any necessary X-rays. All of this gives us an accurate picture of your oral health and allows us to discuss the findings and all possible treatment options with you.

No treatment is usually carried out at this first visit, unless it is to relieve pain or you ask us to.

Why choose us

We take the time to care for our patients in a relaxed but professional atmosphere. Our practice is dedicated to providing all types of dental treatment, from cosmetic procedures to family dentistry.

We protect and maintain your oral health, minimising your risk of dental problems and the need for future treatment.

Our aim is to provide the highest standard of general and specialist dental care at all times.
